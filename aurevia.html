  • Brand Identity

    Build a brand people recognize, remember, and trust.
    AUREVIA creates distinctive and cohesive brand identities that transform ideas into memorable visual experiences. From strategic positioning and logo design to typography, color systems, visual language, and brand guidelines, we develop identities that communicate personality with clarity and confidence.
    Typical deliverables

    • Logo & visual identity
    • Brand color system
    • Typography system
    • Graphic elements & patterns
    • Art direction
    • Brand guidelines
    • Stationery & brand applications

  • UI/UX Design

    Give your brand a clear visual vision.
    AUREVIA designs intuitive, elegant, and purposeful digital experiences that connect users with your product. We combine user-centered thinking with sophisticated visual design to create interfaces that are easy to navigate, engaging to use, and aligned with the brand.
    Typical deliverables

    • Website UI/UX
    • Mobile app interfaces
    • User flows & wireframes
    • Design systems
    • Interactive prototypes
    • Landing pages
    • Responsive interface design

  • Graphic Design

    Turn ideas into powerful visual communication.
    AUREVIA transforms concepts and messages into compelling visual assets designed to capture attention and communicate clearly. Every piece is crafted with intentional typography, composition, imagery, and brand consistency.
    Typical deliverables

    • Social media graphics
    • Marketing materials
    • Posters & advertisements
    • Presentation design
    • Editorial layouts
    • Brochures & flyers
    • Digital campaign assets

  • Creative Direction

    Give your brand a clear visual vision.
    Creative direction is where strategy, storytelling, and aesthetics come together. AUREVIA develops the overarching creative vision behind a brand or campaign, ensuring every visual touchpoint feels intentional, distinctive, and connected.
    Typical deliverables

    • Creative concepts
    • Visual storytelling
    • Campaign direction
    • Moodboards & visual references
    • Photography direction
    • Typography & art direction
    • Campaign visual systems

  • Packaging Architecture

    Design packaging that becomes part of the product experience.
    AUREVIA approaches packaging as more than a container—it is a physical expression of your brand. We develop packaging systems that balance aesthetics, functionality, hierarchy, and recognition to create products that stand confidently on the shelf and remain memorable in the customer's hands.
    Typical deliverables

    • Packaging concepts
    • Structural packaging direction
    • Box & label design
    • Product line architecture
    • Packaging hierarchy
    • Material & finish direction
    • Packaging mockups
